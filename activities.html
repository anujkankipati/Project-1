Basketball is one of my favorite ways to spend my free time. I enjoy playing with friends because it gives me a chance to compete, stay active, and take a break from school and programming.

Food & Friends

One of my favorite things to do with friends is explore new restaurants and food spots. I enjoy trying different kinds of food and finding places that I have never been to before.

Fitness

Going to the Gym

Fitness is an important part of my weekly routine. I try to go to the gym at least three times each week to stay active and continue improving myself.

Staying consistent with the gym has helped me value discipline and making time for goals outside of academics.

Spending time with my family is extremely important to me. Whether we are going somewhere together, watching sports, eating, or simply spending time at home, I value being able to stay connected with the people closest to me.

Family gives me an important balance between school, career goals, and my personal life.
